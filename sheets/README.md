## Handling

The PDFs are excluded by `.gitignore` for local use. No commit, upload or publication was performed. Notes and the manifest can be versioned separately; a fresh clone will not contain the PDFs.

See the [score-skeleton skill](../skills/score-skeleton/SKILL.md). Separate observations, interpretations and practice reductions. An unaccompanied solo staff is not evidence of a complete backing chord progression.
